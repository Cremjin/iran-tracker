Who Checks the Machines?

Every "safe" AI model rests on a hidden workforce of content checkers — the people who label, moderate and rate the data that trains and polices generative systems. They are concentrated in a handful of cities, and they are paid wildly different wages for nearly identical work. Explore the map: red marks the most underpaid hubs, green the best-paid.

Methodology & Notes

Hotspots mark documented hubs of AI content checking — content moderation, data labeling, and RLHF/preference rating — work that filters, annotates and grades the material behind generative AI. Wages are reported ranges from investigative journalism and academic studies; the headline figure is a representative take-home average and may differ from gross or company-quoted pay. Firm market shares and the W-2-vs-contractor splits are illustrative estimates synthesized from industry rankings and reporting, not audited financials — they convey structure, not precision. Demographic notes are drawn from the cited reporting and are partial. The map uses an equirectangular projection; Antarctica is omitted.
